# Refined — Retention & Growth Strategy

*Companion to `Refined_MVP_Structure.md` and `Refined_Features.md`. This is the "why people come back and why they bring others" layer. Premium habit formation, not dark patterns — consistent with the no-gamification, no-spam, no-streak-pressure philosophy.*

---

## 0. The principle

Durable engagement here comes from **ritual + identity + earned progress + social** — not loss-aversion. Fitted's streaks and credit-metering are exactly what we reject. Our retention is defensible because every loop **compounds on personal data only we hold** (your body, your coloring, your closet, your taste). The longer you use Refined, the better it gets and the harder it is to leave.

---

## 1. The core retention model (two nested loops)

**Outer loop — the daily ritual (habit driver).**
Each morning, "today's look" from the closet, weather- and recently-worn aware. The notification is specific and valuable *even unopened* ("בוקר. היום קצת קריר, הכנתי לך לוק שלא לבשת השבוע."). Occasionally it spikes into a peak moment (a detected event, rain tomorrow). **Rule: never an empty "we miss you" push.** This is the single biggest habit lever.

**Inner loop — effort becomes reward (depth driver).**
Every added piece visibly multiplies outfits. Every save/dismiss makes the next suggestion more "you" via `preference_profile`. The app gets *better the more it's used*, which is the opposite of fatigue.

**The compounding layer — earned progress.**
Weekly progress + Style Wrapped (§3). Self-narrative is the stickiest content there is.

---

## 2. The cold-start problem (the #1 churn cliff)

**The risk:** the magic moment is a *one-time* wow. Everything that brings people back needs a *populated closet*. If a user lands in the app with a thin or empty closet, the loop has nothing to run on and they churn by Day 2. Fitted's own screens show the trap: "0 pieces · ₪0.00."

**Fixes (build these into Phase 1, not later):**
- **Blend owned + aspirational in Day-0 looks.** The first looks can mix extracted-from-photo items with a few suggested/shoppable pieces, so the experience feels complete before the closet is full.
- **Quantify the unlock.** "Adding this jacket unlocked 12 new looks." Make closet-building feel like progress, not data entry.
- **Frictionless ramp.** Photo extraction + library import as the default path; Gmail import (Phase 2) for a big one-tap jump.
- **Define first-session success explicitly:** user leaves session one with (a) a Style Identity they believe, (b) at least ~8–10 pieces in the closet, (c) at least one saved look. If a session ends below this, the loop is at risk.

---

## 3. Earned progress & self-insight (the addictive, shareable layer)

**Weekly progress (flagged as the strongest retention mechanic).**
"השבוע הסטייל שלך נעשה עקבי יותר." Real, earned stats: previously-unworn items used, consistency, range. Numbers must feel earned, never inflated.

**Style Wrapped (the highest-leverage future bet).**
A monthly/seasonal recap of how the user's style identity is evolving. This is the Spotify Wrapped lesson: **content about *yourself* is the most returnable and most shareable thing you can make**, and it's defensible because it compounds on data only Refined holds. It is simultaneously a retention hook and an organic-virality engine. I'd prioritize this above most of the future roadmap.

**"Wore it, felt great" feedback.**
Let users log how a look made them feel. The app learns from it *and* clothing becomes an emotional reward loop. Quietly powerful, cheap to build, deeply on-brand for a confidence product.

---

## 4. Virality engines

- **Couple mode** — coordinated looks across two linked wardrobes. The strongest built-in lever: a partner installs to participate. (Data model already reserved for cross-wardrobe outfits.)
- **Style Wrapped sharing** — beautiful, identity-affirming, screenshot-native.
- **Friends (private)** — trusted outfit feedback, not a public feed. Private-first, post-PMF.
- **Referral** — intentionally minimal and **not** credit-driven (the opposite of Fitted). Premium products are referred because they're loved, not because of a bribe.

---

## 5. Monetization sequencing (decide deliberately)

Gate the magic moment → kill activation. Never gate → don't convert. The sequence that protects both:

```
Free magic moment (the wow)  →  soft/late sign-up (save your result)  →  
paywall AT the moment value expands (full closet, daily looks, try-on)
```

Subscription-clean, no credits. Anchor the yearly plan. The paywall should land *after* the user has felt the value and *as* they reach for more, never before the wow.

---

## 6. Ethical guardrails (retention-optimal AND right)

Body analysis + a confidence product means body-image is a live risk. The affirming choice is also the retention choice — shame drives churn, confidence drives return.

- All analysis and copy is **affirming and additive** ("what makes you feel confident"), never deficit-framed ("hide your flaws").
- **No** weight-loss framing. **No** ranking against other users.
- The product's emotional job is to make people feel *more* like themselves, not less.

---

## 7. Priority order (what actually moves retention)

1. **Fix the cold-start** (§2) — without this, nothing else gets a chance to work.
2. **Nail the daily ritual loop** (§1 outer) — the core habit.
3. **Weekly progress** (§3) — the compounding return reason.
4. **Style Wrapped** (§3) — retention + virality in one, and a moat.
5. **Couple mode** (§4) — the virality unlock, once the core loop is proven.

Everything else (try-on, shopping, communities, packing, calendar) layers on after the loop above is real. Build the loop. Then scale it.
